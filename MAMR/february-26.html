:heart: hrt by sorafloof (22042)

Our first map of February, hrt is a relatively recent map by sora. It was uploaded on Christmas Eve and containing simple aesthetically pleasing effects until the drop of the song, where it famously picks up with split hand anchor holds. This technically bumps the map to Hard difficulty via the Awarding Criteria, but should hopefully be easy enough for new players to grasp. It could just make the Beginner region even more confusing... but I honestly couldn't tell you. Regardless, the map was well-received, ironed out over time, and was qualified on the 20th. Congrats to sora on ranking her third effect map and fifth overall! And that's it for February... yep. Only three maps were ranked through that entire dry spell. Luckily things picked up in March, as you may be aware of.
