:heart: hrt by sorafloof (22042)

Our first map of February, hrt is a relatively recent map by sora. It was uploaded on Christmas Eve and containing simple yet pleasing effects until the drop of the song, where it famously picks up with split hand anchor holds that technically bump this map to Hard difficulty, but should hopefully be easy enough for new players to grasp. It could just make Beginner even more confusing... but I honestly couldn't tell you. Regardless, the map was well-received and the holes were ironed out over time and after the map was qualified on the 20th. Congrats to her on ranking her third effect map and fifth map overall! And that's it for February... yep. Only three maps were ranked through that entire dry spell. Luckily things picked up in March, as you may be aware of.
