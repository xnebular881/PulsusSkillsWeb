We are finally back, and this is the oldest edition to be posted to the PSW! Welcome, one and all, to MAMR! Once again, we'll be going over every map that was ranked over the previous month. Three maps were ranked this month, starting with...

👨‍👦 Papaoutai by GlowBlock64 (18862)

By this point, we all know Glow for tech, and this map is no exception. It was originally made in December last year but wasn't pushed for rank until June. It recieved gradual modding since, adding in more ring finger tech in conjunction with the crosshand tech the map originally had. After mods, it went swiftly through qualification status and was ranked on the 9th. Congratulations to Glow on his second ranked map!

💥 U.S.M. by Pongeebob (17060)

Ah, USM. Most of us have heard of this one before, the iconic PSC24 taphold map and sequel to ESM. The map was always going for rank after the tournament, but stayed in modding hell for a long time. Despite this, the core of the map stayed the same. Gradual renewed interest and some tweaks would lead to USM finally landing its spot on the ranked page on the 18th after being qualified on the 10th. Congratulations to Pongee on finally ranking this map!

🍬 Jawbreaker by Phonem (15127)

Phonem's return saw the revival of him ranking maps from his OMORI OST project. Jawbreaker has been the first of these, with the map being an Insane difficulty consisting of consisting of multiple rhythms and chords. The map was qualified on the 17th and ranked on the 26th, with it held up a bit to due timing issues. Congratulations to Phonem on ranking this map, and hopefully we can look forward to more in the future!

Fun fact: this was the first non-Expert map ranked in nearly five months, with Valiant Hero being the most recent one prior on April 28th.
