Welcome to the October edition of MAMR. We only had two maps ranked this month, with both of them being very hard and high-profile. Let's get started.


🔫 We Could Get More Machinegun Psystyle! // Weapon of Mass Destruction by caoutchouc (20094)

Ah yes, Machinegun. The infamous speed challenge and 22 star behemoth. Caou uploaded this map as a WIP in May when the map was mostly finished, and it was swiftly sent to the ANT queue. Despite not recieving ANT mods for a while, the map recieved a lot of attention and plays and became very hyped for rank. and highly starred before it wasn't (iykyk). Congrats to caou on ranking their 28th map and the new hardest ranked map in the game! (rewrite this a bit)


🐶 Mysta Megamix // mystical by rowindigo (18179)

Mysta Megamix was uploaded in early December of last year and was one of a few maps that rowan had an interest in ranking. Featuring crosshand tech and overloads as well as spread streams and unique stream patterns, the map would prove to be incredibly fun even before modding. It took bursts of remotivation to get it modded, and though it was delayed and tweaked slightly, it was able to find its way onto the ranked page as our hardest ranked explicit map with such a mystical song. Congrats to rowan on their first ever ranked map, and on getting the 333rd awarded map!
