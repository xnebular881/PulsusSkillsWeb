Welcome to MAMR: Phonem Edition. Two maps were ranked this month,


🌌 Three Bar Logos by Phonem (14827)

This map as well as our next one were both put in the modding forum with his other OMORI maps when Phonem returned in August. The map passed by the initial modding stages, or lack thereof, until October when the map was sent to the ANT queue. The core of the map remained through ANT mods, with its chords, connected holds, and patterns which make it easy for new players to grasp the game and play with multiple fingers. Fun fact: This is the first Normal difficulty ranked since STHHRESSED OUT all the way back in February, and that map was intended to be an Advanced difficulty. If we're going by intended difficulty, then Valiant Hero's Normal diff would be the most recent one and that was seven months ago, but if we're not counting either of those then it'd be Garyou Tensei... you see how confusing this game is?
