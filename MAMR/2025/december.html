[more intro] As we commemorate the passing of time and leap into a new chapter, now is the perfect time to reflect on the ranked maps of the past two months We ended this year with a bang, with 6 maps getting ranked! Let's reflect on all the maps that were ranked at the end of the year together.


🎎 Ningyou Saiban (人形裁判) by sorafloof (21429)

Ningyou Saiban is a map that should not be an Insane difficulty. It was originally made for S.Q.U.A.R.E.S. as a tech pick, but would eventually be pushed for rank as sora felt proud of it. Featuring split hand chord and anchor tech, the map is one that can be hard to learn but becomes a fun challenge once understood. A modding post was created for the map out of the tournament, which saw more interest shine on the map as it headed into qualification, playtesting, and refining. A shining example of what the complex nature of this game and tech, sora's third ranked map poses a unique challenge amongst not just ranked maps, but tech in general. Congratulations to her as we await her 1,2,3,4th ranked map...
