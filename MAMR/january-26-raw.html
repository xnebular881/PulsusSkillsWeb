🤖 2 HAL 9000 by snekk (20412)

Starting off strong, we have snekk's 10th ranked map and the fourth ranked map from PSC25. Featuring chordstreams and handing tech, it poses a unique challenge in the ranked section. Its popularity was shown when it won a poll to see which map snekk should rank first from the tournament, and so a modding post was created for it on December 5th. After going through the traditional progess of being modded by two ANT members at different times, the map was ranked on the 28th after the removal of a pattern that was slightly hard to play, but was funny. Congrats to snekk on ranking her new hardest map and double digit ranked maps!

🌌 Stellaria // Interstellar by caoutchouc (19490)

Stellaria is a map that has streams. Coming in at 14.73☆, this map is the new fourth hardest ranked map by star rating and the hardest map ranked since Machinegun, dethroning Mysta Megamix. It was the first map qualified in 2026, with that happening after a considerable amount of modding, albeit sporadically. Despite only being ranked on the 9th, this was the final map ranked in January and unknowingly entered us into a real dry spell. This map marks caou's 28th ranked map, which is a big achievement! With more maps colored in caou's signature style on the horizon, we'll have to see what gets ranked next.
